# Awesome-Retail-Workforce-Management

## Top Retail Workforce Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Employee Scheduling, Shift Planning, Demand Forecasting, Time & Attendance, Labor Optimization & Frontline Workforce Tools*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Retail Workforce Management (WFM)**. These systems help retailers create optimized schedules, forecast labor demand, track time and attendance, manage shift swaps, ensure compliance, and improve frontline employee experience.



**Examples** include UKG Workforce Management / Dimensions, Legion Technologies, Quinyx, Deputy, WorkForce Software, Ceridian Dayforce, Reflexis (Zebra), SAP SuccessFactors WFM, Planday, When I Work, and SameSystem (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise retail WFM platforms with AI demand forecasting, complex labor rules, multi-site optimization, and deep POS/HR integrations are commercial. Open-source options include scheduling engines, rostering tools, shift-planning applications, and optimization libraries. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[UKG (Workforce Management / Dimensions)](https://www.ukg.com/)**  

  Leading enterprise workforce management suite combining scheduling, time tracking, HR, and payroll capabilities widely used by large multi-site retailers.



- **[Legion Technologies](https://legion.co/)**  

  AI-native workforce management platform focused on intelligent scheduling, demand forecasting, employee preferences, and engagement for hourly retail and frontline workforces.



- **[Quinyx](https://www.quinyx.com/)**  

  Cloud WFM platform strong in AI-powered demand forecasting, automated schedule optimization, and mobile experience for retail, hospitality, and shift-based industries.



- **[Deputy](https://www.deputy.com/)**  

  Popular scheduling, time tracking, and team communication platform known for ease of use and strong mobile apps, especially among independent and mid-sized retailers.



- **[Ceridian Dayforce](https://www.ceridian.com/)**  

  Unified HCM and workforce management platform offering scheduling, time & attendance, and labor compliance features for retail and other industries.



- **[WorkForce Software, Reflexis (Zebra)](https://www.workforcesoftware.com/)**  

  Enterprise and retail-focused WFM solutions covering advanced scheduling, task management, and store execution.



- **[Planday, When I Work, SameSystem](https://www.planday.com/)**  

  Scheduling and workforce tools popular with smaller chains, franchises, and growing retail operations.



- **[SAP SuccessFactors WFM and other enterprise suites](https://www.sap.com/)**  

  Workforce management capabilities within broader HCM and retail execution platforms.



- **[Other retail WFM platforms](https://www.ukg.com/)**  

  Additional commercial solutions for labor forecasting, shift bidding, and frontline communication.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Staffjoy (historical open-source scheduling)](https://github.com/staffjoy)**  

  Well-known open-source workforce scheduling projects (Suite / v2) that provided auto-scheduling, shift management, and mobile experiences for shift-based teams (legacy but influential).



- **[pyworkforce](https://github.com/rodrigo-arenas/pyworkforce)**  

  Python library for workforce planning, queuing models (Erlang), shift scheduling, rostering, and optimization using constraint programming—useful for building custom labor models.



- **[DutyDock](https://github.com/dutydock/dutydock)**  

  Open-source shift planning and rostering software designed for teams with complex scheduling constraints.



- **[TimeTables](https://github.com/dlsnyder8/TimeTables)**  

  Open-source employee shift scheduling and management application with automatic schedule generation and availability handling.



- **[Workshift](https://github.com/saccofrancesco/workshift)**  

  Desktop application for smart shift scheduling, hour tracking, and export, built for local/self-hosted use.



- **[Team Schedule / follow-the-sun tools](https://github.com/aleksandrrudenko/team-schedule)**  

  Open-source scheduling systems for distributed or multi-timezone teams with workload balancing and on-call rotations (adaptable concepts for retail).



- **[Other shift scheduling & rostering projects](https://github.com/search?q=shift+scheduling+OR+employee+rostering+OR+workforce+scheduling)**  

  Community tools for calendar-based scheduling, availability management, and basic labor optimization.



- **[Optimization & OR-Tools based schedulers](https://github.com/search?q=OR-Tools+scheduling+OR+shift+rostering)**  

  Projects leveraging Google OR-Tools or similar solvers for automated roster generation under constraints.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Time & attendance basics**: Simple clock-in apps and timesheet tools that can feed larger systems.

- **Mobile-first schedule viewers**: Open web or PWA front-ends for employees to view and swap shifts.

- **Demand forecasting helpers**: Time-series libraries (Prophet, etc.) applied to sales or traffic data for labor planning.

- **Compliance rule engines**: Scripts that check schedules against labor laws, break rules, or union constraints.

- **Integration middleware**: n8n or custom connectors linking POS data to scheduling logic.

- Spreadsheet + script hybrids still widely used by smaller retail operators for basic rostering.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Use **pyworkforce** or OR-Tools-based solvers for the optimization core, pair with an open-source rostering UI (DutyDock, TimeTables, or custom), and add time-tracking components.  

Commercial platforms (UKG, Legion, Quinyx, Deputy, Dayforce, etc.) deliver retail-specific demand forecasting, multi-site labor optimization, complex rule engines, employee apps, and compliance features that require substantial engineering to approximate.  

Many smaller retailers start with open-source or lightweight scheduling tools and move to commercial WFM as they scale locations and complexity.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Workforce management systems handle employee data, schedules, wages, and compliance with labor laws. Accuracy, privacy, fairness in scheduling, and legal compliance are critical responsibilities.

- Open-source scheduling tools offer transparency and cost savings but require careful configuration for labor rules, scalability, and reliability. They are rarely full replacements for enterprise retail WFM platforms in multi-site or highly regulated environments.
